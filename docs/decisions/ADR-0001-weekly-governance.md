# ADR 0001 Weekly governance and evidence

## Status

Accepted on 2026-10-07.

## Decision

From W2, all planned work is represented by Issues in a weekly milestone. All changes reach `main` through a reviewed PR. Every week has two versioned Word/PDF submissions, a Planned-Done-Evidence-Problems-Next week record, and explicit member allocation. Releases/tags are created at W4, W8, W11 and W14.

## Consequences

The repository becomes the auditable source of progress; Drive is only the distribution location for large artifacts and submitted PDFs. Small structured results are committed; large data/models remain external with a manifest, checksum and stable link.
